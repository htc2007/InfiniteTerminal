Access your bound chests from anywhere with the Infinite Terminal.

1. Hold the Wireless Terminal Receiver and left-click a nearby chest to bind it (click a bound chest again to unbind). Bind as many chests as you like.
2. Use the Wireless Terminal to open an aggregated interface: deposit, loot, quick-stack and sort across every bound chest.
3. While the Wireless Terminal is in your inventory, all ingredients inside bound chests are available for crafting.

Bindings to removed chests are flagged as invalid automatically.
